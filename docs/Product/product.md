### V5 
The UX Philosophy: "The Quiet Mirror"
Cue is not a coach, a boss, or a cheerleader. It is a Mirror.
* Non-Judgmental: We never use words like "bad," "fail," or
  "improvement." We use "Observation," "Pattern," and "Correlation."
* Low Friction/High Signal: The user should feel a "click" of
  realization in 2 seconds of looking at the screen, then put the phone
  away.
* Agency: We provide the "Why," but the user decides the "What to do."

Product Framing: From "Events" to "Rhythms"
In V4, we proved the data is reliable. In V5, we transition the product
from a Logbook (this happened) to a Rhythm (this is who you are).
* Pattern Awareness: We aren't showing data points; we are showing
  Behavioral Gravity.
* The "Contextual Whisper": The UX should feel like a tap on the
  shoulder exactly when the user is about to drift into an old pattern.

Emotional Tone: "The Stoic Observer"
The tone is calm, objective, and slightly detached.
* Standard: "High phone usage detected. In the past, this has correlated
  with delayed afternoon sessions."
* Avoid: "You've been on your phone too much! Try to study now." (This
  triggers guilt and app-avoidance).

V5 Design Directives

1. The "Today Hint" (The Situational Awareness)
* Framing: This is "Situational Awareness."
* UX Tone: It should only appear when the "Gravity" of a pattern is
  pulling on the user right now. If there's no match, the screen is
  empty and peaceful.
* Visual: It shouldn't look like an "Alert." It should look like a
  "Thought."

2. Pattern Strength (The Reliability)
* Framing: Instead of "Confidence Scores" (technical), we use "Clarity"
  or "Weight".
* UX Tone:
    * Emerging: "We're starting to see a connection..."
    * Established: "This pattern is becoming clear."
    * Strong: "This is a consistent part of your rhythm."

3. The Timeline (The Evolution)
* Framing: This is not a list of mistakes. It is the Story of the
  Pattern.
* UX Tone: It shows how a pattern was born, how it was reinforced,
  and—eventually—how it fades as the user changes.
